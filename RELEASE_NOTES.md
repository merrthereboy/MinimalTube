# MinimalTube v1.1.0 - Luxury Dark Mode & Account Sync

Welcome to the **MinimalTube v1.1.0** release! This major update brings a refined visual interface and seamless YouTube account synchronization.

### 🌟 What's New in v1.1.0:
- **Luxury Dark Mode Redesign:**
  - Strict 80/15/5 aesthetic: `#0D0D11` background, `#18181F` rounded cards, and vibrant Brand Gradient accents (`#00F2FE` Cyan &rarr; `#FF2E93` Magenta).
  - Clean vector iconography with zero emojis.
- **5-Tab Navigation System:**
  - **TRANG CHỦ:** Horizontal category filter chips (All, Live, Music, Gaming, News, Tech, Trending) + recommended feed.
  - **SHORTS:** Dedicated vertical feed for YouTube Shorts.
  - **FANS ZONE:** Subscriptions feed with real-time creator updates.
  - **VIDEO CỦA TÔI:** Persistent local watch history and liked collection.
  - **TÔI:** Profile overview, Google login status, and player settings.
- **YouTube Account Synchronization:**
  - Sign in directly with your Google Account to sync subscriptions and history.
  - 100% private: Credentials and session cookies are preserved locally on your phone.
- **Persistent Release Keystore:**
  - All future updates are signed with the same key, allowing you to install over previous versions ("cài đè") without signature mismatch errors.
- **Upgraded Extraction Engine:**
  - Powered by NewPipeExtractor `v0.27.1` with enhanced stream resolution and resilience against YouTube signature cipher changes.
- **Optimized Video Playback:**
  - ExoPlayer hardware acceleration with texture view rendering (no black screen glitches).
